# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

BoltiToli exists to help teachers and children. We want everyone who contributes, tests or uses it to feel
welcome and respected, regardless of age, background, language, ability, gender identity, caste, religion,
experience or appearance.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and constructive, especially with people who are new or learning.
- Respect different viewpoints; disagree with ideas, never with people.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully, and credit others for their work.
- Put the interests of teachers and children first in product decisions.
- Protect privacy: never share personal data of teachers, children or schools.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Sexual language or imagery, threats or intimidation
- Deliberately disrupting discussions or the project

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions and any event where someone
represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the project team privately. Reports are handled
confidentially and as quickly as possible.

## Consequences

Maintainers may remove comments or contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict anyone who
breaks this code.
